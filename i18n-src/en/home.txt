183	Emergency lock and door opening in&nbsp;<span class="accent">Bilbao</span>: what to know before calling a locksmith
184	If your front door will not open, the first step is to work out what the problem is and avoid damaging the lock with improvised attempts. Below we explain how emergency door opening works, when a mechanism needs replacing and what to have ready before the locksmith arrives.
185	<h3>Can a door be opened without damaging the lock?</h3>
186	<p>In many cases the front door can be opened while keeping the lock and the door leaf intact. The result depends on how the door is built, the type of cylinder, the condition of the mechanism and the reason it is blocked. For example, a door that has slammed shut and a faulty multi-point lock need different approaches.</p>
187	<p>The CerrajeroUCR locksmith first inspects the door and assesses whether it can be opened without damage. If restoring access means damaging or replacing some parts, the specialist will explain the situation and agree the steps with you before starting. Find out more on the <a href="{url:svc-puertas}">emergency door opening</a> page.</p>
188	<h3>What to do if your front door is jammed?</h3>
189	<p>Do not force the key, hit the lock or try to prise the door open with whatever is at hand. This can make the fault worse, damage the cylinder and increase the cost of the repair. Check whether a sagging door leaf is stopping it from closing and whether the key shows any signs of damage.</p>
190	<p>If the mechanism still does not work, contact a locksmith. Tell them whether the key turns, whether the handle opens the door and what kind of lock you have. With this information the specialist can assess the job in advance. If the cause is a worn mechanism, a <a href="{url:svc-reparacion}">door lock repair</a> will help.</p>
191	<h3>When does a lock need replacing after opening?</h3>
192	<p>You do not have to replace the lock after every emergency opening. If the mechanism is sound and the door was opened without damage, it is usually enough to check it works. Replacement may be needed if the cylinder is worn, a key has broken inside, the mechanism is damaged or it keeps sticking.</p>
193	<p>Losing your keys or moving into a new flat is a different matter. Even if the door opens normally, a new cylinder ensures nobody can use old sets of keys. The locksmith will tell you whether replacing the cylinder is enough or the whole lock needs changing. Find out more about <a href="{url:svc-cambio}">lock and cylinder replacement</a>.</p>
194	<h3>How to prepare for the locksmith’s visit?</h3>
195	<p>When you call, give your address in Bilbao or Biscay and the type of property, and briefly describe the problem: the door slammed shut, the keys are lost, the lock is jammed or the key is damaged. If you can, send a photo of the door and the lock — it helps with a preliminary estimate.</p>
196	<p>Have a document or other proof of your right to access the property ready. If your documents are inside, let the dispatcher know in advance. Before starting, the locksmith will check that you are authorised, inspect the lock and agree the price and any additional costs. You can send a request <a href="#request">using the form below</a> or by phone.</p>

202	<p>Emergency door lock opening costs from €45, car opening from €45 and safe opening from €60. The final price depends on the type of door and lock, the time of day and the area; the dispatcher will tell you the call-out fee when you call. The locksmith gives the exact amount after inspecting the lock and before starting work — he will not begin without your consent.</p>
203	How long does it take for the locksmith to arrive?
204	<p>It depends on the area, the time of day and how busy the on-call locksmiths are. The dispatcher gives you an approximate arrival time as soon as you call and warns you in advance if the locksmith is delayed. We reach central Bilbao districts fastest. Travel to more distant Greater Bilbao municipalities, such as Muskiz or Larrabetzu, takes longer.</p>
205	Can a front door be opened without damage?
206	<p>In most cases, yes — especially if the door has slammed shut on the latch and the cylinder is in good condition. With armoured doors and high-security locks it is sometimes impossible to save the mechanism. We aim to open the lock without damage, and if that is not possible, we explain why beforehand and agree the next steps and the price with you.</p>
207	Is the lock opening service available 24 hours a day?
208	<p>Yes. We take requests and come out 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including weekends and public holidays. At night, from 22:00 to 08:00, and at weekends and on holidays a surcharge applies — the dispatcher tells you the amount when you call, before the locksmith sets off.</p>
209	Can you open a flat if the keys are inside?
210	<p>Yes, it’s one of the most common situations. If the door has slammed shut with the key left in the lock on the inside, the locksmith will choose the method that suits that particular case. After opening, we will ask you to show a document proving you live at the address: DNI, NIE, a rental contract or a utility bill.</p>
211	What should I do if the key breaks in the lock?
212	<p>Do not try to pull the broken piece out with a hairpin or glue: you could push it further in and damage the cylinder. If the door is open, do not close it. Call a locksmith: he will extract the piece with a special tool, check the mechanism and, if the cylinder is damaged, offer to replace it on the spot. Key extraction costs from €60.</p>
213	What documents are needed to open a flat or a car?
214	<p>For a flat or house, a photo ID (DNI, NIE or passport) and proof of your link to the address: a certificate of registration (empadronamiento), a rental contract, an escritura (title deed) or a utility bill. For a car, the permiso de circulación (vehicle registration) or other vehicle documents. If the documents are inside, we check them straight after opening. In doubtful situations we work in the presence of the police.</p>
215	How much does it cost to open a lock at night?

We tell you the full amount, surcharge included, when you call — before the locksmith sets off — so a night call-out doesn’t end in an unexpected bill.</p>
217	Can the lock be replaced straight after opening?
218	<p>Yes. The locksmith carries popular cylinders and locks with different security ratings, so we usually replace them during the same visit. If your door needs a special model, for example for an armoured door, we will source it and arrange a second visit. We tell you the price of the parts before fitting them.</p>
219	Do you open armoured doors and electronic locks?
220	<p>Yes, we work with armoured doors (puertas acorazadas), multi-point, combination and electronic locks. These are more complex jobs: prices start at €150 and depend on the model and security rating. For an electronic lock, tell us the model and what exactly is wrong — a flat battery, a malfunction or a mechanical fault.</p>

The photos are easy to take on your phone: outside, a close-up of the cylinder; inside, the handle and the latch. The locksmith will identify the type of mechanism and give you an approximate price. The final price is confirmed on site after inspection — before work begins.</p>
