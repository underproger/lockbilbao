204	What to do if you can’t open your flat’s front door?
205	When the door won’t open, the main thing is not to damage the lock or the door leaf. Here’s how to assess the situation before the locksmith arrives, when the lock can be saved and what to have ready.
206	<h3>What to do if the door has slammed shut with the keys inside?</h3>
207	<p>First check whether there is another way in: a spare set with relatives, neighbours or the building manager. Don’t try to prise the door open with a knife, a plastic card or a screwdriver — it’s easy to damage the door leaf, the seal and the latch, and the call-out will cost more.</p>
208	<p>Call a locksmith and tell them whether the door is just on the latch or locked with a key — the opening method depends on it. See the <a href="#prices">price table</a> for guide prices.</p>
209	<h3>Why won’t the key turn in the door lock?</h3>
210	<p>The most common causes are a worn cylinder, dirt in the mechanism, a bent key or a sagging door that makes the bolt catch on the strike plate. Sometimes the lock jams after someone tries to open it with the wrong key.</p>
211	<p>Don’t use force: a key broken in the cylinder will make opening harder. The locksmith will find the exact cause during diagnosis. If the cause is wear, a <a href="{url:svc-reparacion}">door lock repair</a> will help.</p>
212	<h3>Can a flat be opened without replacing the lock?</h3>
213	<p>Yes, if the mechanism is sound and the door has slammed shut on the latch or is locked with a standard cylinder. Replacement is needed when the cylinder is broken or worn, a piece of key is stuck inside or the lock cannot be opened any other way.</p>
214	<p>In that case, the locksmith explains in advance why the mechanism cannot be saved and tells you the price of a new cylinder. Find out more in the <a href="#no-damage">opening without damage</a> section.</p>
215	<h3>Should you change the lock after losing your keys?</h3>
216	<p>Getting back into your flat and keeping it secure are two different things. If your keys were lost together with documents or a key ring showing your address, a stranger could use them.</p>
217	<p>In that case it’s best to replace the cylinder straight away: it’s quicker and cheaper than replacing the whole lock, and the old keys will no longer work. Find out more about <a href="{url:svc-cambio}">lock cylinder replacement</a>.</p>
218	<h3>What should you have ready before the locksmith arrives?</h3>
219	<p>The exact address with the floor and entry code, a short description of the problem and, if possible, a photo of the lock from outside — it makes it easier to estimate the price in advance. Have a document proving your right of access ready: DNI, NIE or passport and a document with your address. If your documents are inside, let the dispatcher know — the locksmith will check them straight after opening. See which documents are accepted in the <a href="#documents">documents</a> section.</p>
