198	What to do if your safe won’t open?
199	When a safe won’t open, the key thing is not to make matters worse: many safes lock out after several wrong attempts, and brute force can jam the mechanism for good. Here’s what to check yourself and when to call a locksmith.
200	<h3>What to do if you’ve forgotten the safe code?</h3>
201	<p>Don’t try combinations one after another: many electronic safes lock out for a while after several wrong attempts, and repeated errors extend the lockout. Look for the manual and your notes — many models have a master code or an emergency key.</p>
202	<p>If you have neither the code nor the emergency key, call a locksmith and give the make and model of the safe — they’re usually shown on the door. The model makes it easier to tell whether the safe can be opened without damage.</p>
203	<h3>Why won’t the electronic safe respond to the buttons?</h3>
204	<p>Most often the batteries are flat. Many models have an external emergency power connector or a back-up mechanical key under a decorative cover. If emergency power can’t be connected, the locksmith will open the safe and replace the batteries.</p>
205	<p>If the safe beeps but won’t open, the electronics may have failed or the bolt may have jammed. Don’t bang the door or yank the handle — it’s easy to damage the mechanism.</p>
206	<h3>Can a safe be opened without drilling?</h3>
207	<p>Yes, if its design allows it: with flat batteries, a back-up key or a working mechanical combination lock. High-grade burglar-resistant safes are designed to resist opening, and the lock can’t always be saved.</p>
208	<p>If drilling can’t be avoided, the locksmith will explain why in advance and tell you the price of a new lock. Find out more in the <a href="#no-damage">opening without damage</a> section.</p>
209	<h3>What to do after the safe has been opened?</h3>
210	<p>Set a new code and check the lock works. If the lock had to be drilled, fit a new one — otherwise the safe will no longer protect its contents. Write the code down and keep the note away from the safe.</p>
211	<p>If the safe keys were lost together with your house keys, also think about <a href="{url:svc-cambio}">replacing the front door cylinder</a>.</p>
