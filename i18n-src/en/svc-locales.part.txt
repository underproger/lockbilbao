198	What to do if your office or shop won’t open?
199	A locked shop or office means missed meetings and lost customers. Here’s how to quickly assess the situation, what to prepare for the locksmith and how to protect the premises after losing the keys.
200	<h3>Office door won’t open: what to check first?</h3>
201	<p>Try all the keys on the bunch and ask colleagues who has a spare set. Check whether the door is locked from inside with a second lock or bolt — this happens if the last person left through the service entrance.</p>
202	<p>Don’t force the key: a key broken in the cylinder will make opening harder. Call a locksmith and describe what’s happening with the lock. See the <a href="#prices">price table</a> for guide prices.</p>
203	<h3>Why does a metal shutter lock jam?</h3>
204	<p>Metal shutter locks (<span lang="es">cierres metálicos</span>) are outdoors: dirt, water and sand get into them. The mechanism gradually wears out, and one day the key stops turning.</p>
205	<p>To prevent this, clean the shutter lock from time to time and lubricate it with a dry lubricant. If it’s already sticking, a <a href="{url:svc-reparacion}">lock repair</a> will help — before it jams completely.</p>
206	<h3>What to do if an employee loses the keys or leaves?</h3>
207	<p>Replace the cylinders as soon as possible: it’s inexpensive and quick, and the old keys will no longer work. If the premises have several doors, you can fit cylinders keyed alike.</p>
208	<p>Find out more about choosing cylinders on the <a href="{url:svc-cambio}">lock and cylinder replacement</a> page.</p>
209	<h3>What to do after a break-in attempt?</h3>
210	<p>Don’t touch the door or lock and call the police on 112 straight away. Photograph the damage: the photos and the police report (<span lang="es">denuncia</span>) will be needed for the insurance claim.</p>
211	<p>Once the damage has been recorded, the locksmith will open the premises, replace the damaged lock and suggest how to improve security — for example, by <a href="{url:svc-instalacion}">fitting an extra lock</a>.</p>
213	<p>The exact address, the type of door and locks and the contact details of whoever will meet the locksmith. Have a photo ID ready and proof of your right to the premises. If you’re an employee, let the owner know — the locksmith may ask them to confirm the job. See which documents are accepted in the <a href="#documents">documents</a> section.</p>
